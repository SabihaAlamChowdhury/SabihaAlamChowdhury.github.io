Research interests

  • Neurosymbolic AI

    Joining neural perception with logic so models can explain how they reached an answer.

  • Multimodal Deep Learning

    Models that combine vision and language, such as visual question answering.

  • Trustworthy Systems

    AI and software that are reliable, transparent and safe for the people who depend on them.

  • Cybersecurity in Digital Twins

    Threat analysis for virtual replicas of hospitals, factories and critical infrastructure.

  • Intelligent Automation

    Simulation and optimization that remove bottlenecks from real-world systems like logistics.
